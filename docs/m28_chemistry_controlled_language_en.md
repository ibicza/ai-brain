# M-28 Controlled Chemistry Language: EN

Exact supported forms include:

- `What is the atomic number of oxygen?`
- `What is the symbol of sodium?`
- `Calculate the molar mass of H2SO4.`
- `Show the composition of Ca(OH)2.`
- `How many mol are in 36 g of H2O?`
- `What is the mass of 2 mol of CO2 in g?`
- `How many molecules are in 0.5 mol of H2O?`

Missing arguments yield clarification. Out-of-scope chemistry is unsupported. A calculation plus save/remember intent is composite and is never partially executed.
