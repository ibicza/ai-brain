# BIPM Mole Mapping

M-28.2 selected **Outcome B** for BIPM. The official SI Brochure 9th edition, version 4.01 PDF is frozen and hash-bound. The brochure metadata, mole definition, Avogadro constant, unit, and exactness statement remain a reviewed field mapping because robust deterministic PDF extraction was not established.

The derivation method is `REVIEWED_MANUAL_MAPPING`. Its evidence records carry reviewed page/section locators, exact canonical values, implementation/policy hashes, and reviewer identity.

Approval `approval_bipm_si_mole_2026` is immutable and content-bound. Model approval and blank reviewers are rejected. This limitation is explicit in the domain manifest.
