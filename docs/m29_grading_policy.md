# M-29 Grading Policy

Final answers use exact Decimal and typed dimensions. Exact conversions include g/kg, mol/mmol, and g/mol/kg/mol. Formula maps are order-independent; interval midpoints are not equivalent to intervals.

Step sequences are compared by operation, numeric operands, output, unit, and graph order. A correct final with invalid submitted steps is marked `CORRECT_FINAL_UNVERIFIED_STEPS`. The 10,000-case acceptance agreement was 1.0000; wrong dimensions and invalid inputs accepted were zero.
