# M-29 Hint Policy

The immutable five-level ladder is orient, next step, substitution, partial calculation, and full solution. Levels 1-4 omit the final value, equivalent final expressions, hidden fields, and later-node outputs. Level 5 is the verified full explanation.

Unambiguous diagnoses select bounded targeted prompts; ambiguous cases use the general next graph step. The gate checked 2,000 sequences / 10,000 artifacts with zero early leakage, unsupported values, or wrong diagnosis targeting.
