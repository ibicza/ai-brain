## Research boundary

[Outlier Exposure](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.04606) motivates diverse unsupported
training examples; this closed UNKNOWN head is not its full anomaly detector.
[Attention is not Explanation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.10186) cautions against
treating an attention map as a faithful explanation. Here the task-scoped loss
is an experimental training intervention, not proof of foreground understanding;
acceptance is determined by held outputs and preserved refusal/error criteria.

For a later contour/pattern stage, [Geirhos et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.12231)
demonstrate texture-versus-shape cue conflict in ImageNet CNNs and improve shape
bias with changed training data. This suggests testing independently varied
contour/texture cues here, not assuming that simply adding parameters solves
the observed failures. [Their later OOD study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.07411)
also distinguishes overall robustness from agreement on individual image errors.
These are research motivations, not replications or evidence about this small
own model. No V21/V22 settings were changed after reviewing these papers.
